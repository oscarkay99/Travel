About the programme

Factory and warehouse work

The Czech Republic is the currently active destination in Rogernort’s work-abroad programme. The programme covers factory worker and warehouse worker roles without a specialist skills requirement.

  • 01 / FACTORY

    Factory worker roles

    Discuss the current opportunities with our team. Ask for the employer, location, duties and contract terms for any proposed role.

  • 02 / WAREHOUSE

    Warehouse worker roles

    Ask about available warehouse roles and the terms that apply, including working hours, pay and any accommodation arrangements.

  • 03 / PREPARATION

    Application support

    Start with a free consultation to review the programme requirements and understand the application and document steps.

This is a programme information page, not a confirmed job vacancy or offer. Employment decisions rest with the employer; work permit and visa decisions rest with the relevant authorities.

Before applying

Who can apply?

Rogernort’s stated programme criteria are:

  • Applicants must be between 21 and 55 years old.
  • A valid passport is required to proceed.
  • No specialist skills are required for the programme.
  • Applicants banned from Europe cannot apply under the programme.

These are programme criteria, not a complete statement of immigration requirements. Discuss your circumstances with our adviser before proceeding.

Your preparation checklist

Documents to prepare

  • Passport
  • Ghana Card
  • CV
  • Birth certificate
  • Police report

Our adviser will explain the document review and submission process, including when your passport number is required. Begin your enquiry with your questions; you do not need to send document copies in your first message.

Understand the commitment

Fees and payment timing

The stated complete programme cost is EUR 6,000. The first payment is EUR 3,000, due immediately after the applicant submits their documents.

The initial consultation and application are free. Before submitting documents and making a payment, request the written agreement, an itemised explanation of what the programme cost covers, and the timing of the remaining payment.

Refund eligibility

Rogernort’s stated refund eligibility applies only if the client’s offer is not received within the stated three-to-six-month period. Ask our team to explain the exact deadline, refund procedure, required evidence and payment terms in writing before proceeding.

Confirm payment instructions directly with an authorised Rogernort adviser. The official GCB account belongs to Rogernort; account details are not published on this page.

Questions before you commit

Czech Republic work-abroad FAQs

Are jobs or visas guaranteed?

No. Applying to the programme does not guarantee employment, a work permit or a visa. Confirm current opportunities and employer terms with our team. The employer and relevant authorities make the final decisions.

Do I need previous specialist experience?

The current programme covers factory and warehouse worker roles without a specialist skills requirement. Ask our adviser about the duties and any employer-specific requirements for the role under discussion.

Can I proceed without a valid passport?

No. A valid passport is required to proceed with this programme. You may contact us with questions before you are ready to submit an application.

How long does processing take?

Rogernort’s indicative programme processing period is three to six months. This is not a guarantee of an offer, appointment, permit or visa within that time. Ask your adviser about your case and the written programme terms.

What does the EUR 6,000 cover?

The confirmed total programme cost is EUR 6,000, but a full breakdown is not published here. Ask our team for written confirmation of inclusions and exclusions, including any flights, insurance, accommodation or other expenses, before paying.

Can I apply for another European country?

The Czech Republic is the only currently active country in this work-abroad programme. Our worldwide visa assistance service is separate and does not mean employment programmes are available in every country.
