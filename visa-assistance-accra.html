Practical help with your application

Three ways we can assist

We offer visa application assistance for all countries. The documents, application process and appointment options depend on your destination and reason for travel. We’ll discuss the support available for your particular application before you proceed.

  • 01 / DOCUMENTS

    Document review

    Get help reviewing your supporting documents and identifying information you may need to provide for your destination and travel purpose.

  • 02 / APPLICATION

    Application forms

    Get assistance completing visa application forms using your own accurate information. Review the details before your application is submitted.

  • 03 / APPOINTMENTS

    Appointment booking

    Get help navigating appointment booking where it is required. Appointment dates depend on availability through the relevant booking system.

Rogernort provides application assistance. Visa decisions are made by the relevant embassy or immigration authority; our assistance does not guarantee approval or a particular appointment date.

Before you apply

Visa assistance questions

Which countries do you provide visa assistance for?

We offer assistance for destinations worldwide. Tell us your destination and reason for travel so we can confirm the process and support for your application. Requirements and appointment availability differ by country.

Can you help if I have already started my application?

Tell us which steps you have completed and where you need help. Our team will review your enquiry and confirm whether document review, form assistance or appointment booking is appropriate.

How much does visa assistance cost?

Contact us for a quote based on your destination and the assistance you need. Ask for a breakdown of Rogernort’s service fees and any separate application or appointment charges before proceeding.

Can you guarantee a visa or an appointment date?

No. Approval is decided by the relevant embassy or immigration authority, and appointment dates depend on available slots. We help you prepare and navigate the application process.

How long will my application take?

There is no single timeline for all destinations. Ask our team about your planned travel dates and the process for your case. Processing times and appointment availability are outside Rogernort’s control.

Visit The Base, New-Legon, Adenta, Monday–Friday, 9am–5pm. Call or WhatsApp +233 55 949 9248 to discuss your enquiry.
