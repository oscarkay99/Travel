Questions about your Dubai holiday

How much is a Dubai holiday package from Accra?

Rogernort’s indicative price is USD 1,200–2,000 per person for the seven-day package departing from Accra. Your final quote depends on your requested dates and availability. Contact us to confirm the amount before booking.

Does the package include flights and a visa?

Yes. The package includes visa arrangements, a flight, hotel accommodation, breakfast and a tour. Ask our team for the exact flight and hotel details. Including visa arrangements does not guarantee visa approval.

Is there a fixed departure date?

No fixed departure date is advertised here. Send us your preferred dates so our team can check the available arrangements and provide a quote.

Is this a work-abroad programme?

No. This page describes a Dubai holiday package. For information about our separate work-abroad service, visit the Czech Republic work-abroad programme.

Yes. Visit Rogernort Travel & Tour at The Base, New-Legon, Adenta, Monday–Friday, 9am–5pm. You can also call or WhatsApp +233 55 949 9248.
